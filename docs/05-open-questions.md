## Product

### Q1. Keep the offline (non-AI) study-card fallback?
- **Context:** When the AI is busy, over quota, times out or the phone is
  offline, `OfflineStudyMaker` builds simple cards from definitions it finds
  in the notes ("X is Y", "Term: meaning"). The owner asked "whats does made
  offline means? is it generated by ai?" and was told it is **not** AI. The
  owner then reported "it always run the offline build" (fixed by changing
  the model order).
- **Options raised:** keep it as is; rename it (for example "Quick cards
  (no AI)"); disable it so failures show an error instead.
- **Current behaviour:** kept. The material shows a "Made offline" banner.

### Q2. Tagline "★ PLANT EDITION ★"
- It comes from the "Study Buddy — Plant Edition" era. It is still shown
  under the kuwaGO logo (`AppInfo.tagline`). Keep it, change it, or drop it?

### Q3. How much should a plant grow per session once rounds exist?
- The plan proposed **one stage per completed focus round**, so the default
  4 rounds would grow a whole plant. The owner never answered.
- **Current behaviour:** one stage per completed session (there are no
  rounds).

### Q4. Rounds and breaks: wanted?
- They are in the owner's original spec (session setup) and in the plan.
  The owner later asked only for a single focus-time setter on Home, which
  was built. Not confirmed whether breaks and rounds are still wanted.

### Q5. What should the Android back button do during a session?
- The plan said back would *minimize* the session (Home shows "RETURN TO
  SESSION").
- **Current behaviour:** back asks "give up?". This was built to fix the
  orphaned-timer bug.
- Revisit this together with timer persistence.

### Q6. Where else should kuwago appear?
- Suggested: harvest popup, wilted-plant comfort, sleeping when paused,
  journal / AI "thinking", Garden sign, Profile. The owner chose only Home
  (plus loading and login), so the other placements are unconfirmed.
- Also unconfirmed: whether kuwago replaces the "garden companion" (bee,
  mushroom or sprout spirit) that the plan left for the owner to pick.

### Q7. Real daily streak vs. "in a row"
- The streak counts **sessions in a row**, and the label says "in a row".
  The plan proposed a real day streak. Not confirmed.

### Q8. Minimum session length
- The focus setter allows 1 minute, which makes it easy to farm growth.
  Should there be a minimum, or should short sessions give less growth or
  XP? Not discussed with the owner.

### Q9. Push Reminders
- The toggle on Profile is UI only. Build it (what message? when?) or
  remove it?
